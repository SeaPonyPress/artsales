About Sea Pony Press

Sea Pony Press is a community of makers committed to the Tongass and our unique ways of life — forged through acts of creative expression. We support artist residencies, community workshops, and collaborative projects that celebrate and defend Southeast Alaska.

Sea Pony Press is a program of the Sitka Conservation Society, whose mission is protecting the Tongass and supporting thriving communities across Southeast Alaska. Sea Pony Press is part of SCS's Living Wilderness Fund, with support from the Sustainable Southeast Partnership and the Richard L & Diane M Block Foundation.
